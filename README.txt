Mohamed Sharallah portfolio, with two selectable website designs.

Open index.html for the new Cinema design. Open original-design.html for the original site design. Both pages show two rotating 3D choices in the hero: tap the torus knot to switch to the original design, or tap the 3D cinema camera to switch to the Cinema design. The camera fires a pulsing light beam with floating film-frame fragments and spinning reels. The text links under the objects also switch designs.

Upload every file in this folder together to the same GitHub Pages publishing folder. Keep design-switcher.js, both HTML pages, portfolio.pdf, and all video and poster files together.

The 3D objects use Three.js from jsDelivr and take their colors from each page's palette. If WebGL or the CDN is unavailable, the design links still work. The Cinema carousel opens on the personal finance edit and rotates every 3 seconds. The new Trail video is its last slide. Videos autoplay muted; visitors can pause rotation, use the arrows or dots, and turn on sound with the controls.



A large animated dragon and fire artwork sits behind both portfolio designs. The effect adapts for mobile and respects reduced-motion settings.
